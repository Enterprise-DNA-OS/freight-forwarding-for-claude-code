# CargoWise target research

Checked 9 October 2026. Family: international freight forwarding, new to the rebuild registry. Distinct from the road freight build (TransVirtual), which covers consignments, runs and proof of delivery for carriers, not house shipments, ocean and air bookings, border rules and job profit.

## The bill

CargoWise moved most customers to Value Pack pricing from 1 December 2025: no seat fees, one automation fee per shipment. The [CargoWise Value Pack Community Pricing page](https://www.cargowise.com/lp/cargowise-value-pack/cargowise-value-pack-community-pricing/) (version 26.10) lists, in USD, per shipment:

| Forwarding Value Pack | FCL/FTL | LCL/LTL/AIR/NON-FCL |
|---|---|---|
| Import shipment (including any domestic legs) | 19.95 | 13.30 |
| Export shipment (including any domestic legs) | 12.75 | 8.35 |
| Gateway, transit, cross trade, co-load master to forwarder | 3.95 | 2.63 |

The same page lists cloud read-only access at USD 2,500 per service per month, a USD 250 per instance per month minimum for customer-hosted instances, extended credit terms at 2 to 6 percent, and a surcharge for staying on an old version. The [Value Pack FAQ](https://cargowise.com/cargowise-value-pack/cargowise-value-pack-faq/) says the fee can be passed to the customer as a disbursement.

Industry reporting: the Journal of Commerce reported forwarders expecting CargoWise costs to rise 20 to 50 percent under the new model, some more than double; [The Loadstar](https://theloadstar.com/forwarders-are-rethinking-cargowise-what-are-their-options/) reports small and mid-sized forwarders reassessing. These are reports, not invoices.

## Who pays for this kind of system

- NZ and AU freight forwarders and customs brokers moving a few hundred to a few thousand shipments a year.
- Importers and exporters with their own logistics desk.
